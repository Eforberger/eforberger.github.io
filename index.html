Elsa Forberger

I'm a software engineer at Blue Origin working on Blue Alchemist, a In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) initiative to produce oxygen and solar panels from lunar regolith. My work involves everything from robotics and controls to sensor calibration and testing.

Before joining Blue Origin, I did my B.S. in Computer Science and M.S. in Robotics at the University of Minnesota, where I was advised by Professor Maria Gini in the Next Generation Robotics Lab. I also spent some time in the Interactive Robotics and Vision Lab working on dynamic gesture recognition for AUVs under Professor Junaed Sattar.

I'm interested in robust, adaptive control to address changes in capability arising from robot degradation, and how those capabilites can inform high level task selection and planning for a wider multi-agent team.
